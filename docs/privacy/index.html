Orienta Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Orienta is a trip-assistance product operated by Zhenxiang Jian (sole proprietor). This page explains how we handle information when an airport or airline operator texts you a one-time Orienta invite link.

What we collect

When you agree to receive an invite text, the operator enters the mobile number you provided for that trip. We use that number only to send the invite SMS and related delivery logs (for example, whether the carrier accepted the message).

How we use your mobile number

Your mobile number is used solely to deliver transactional Orienta trip messages that you consented to receive. We do not sell, rent, or share mobile numbers with third parties for their marketing. We do not buy or scrape phone lists.

Message frequency

You will typically receive one SMS per trip: a short instruction plus a unique claim link. Additional messages are sent only if you or the operator request a resend of that same invite. Message and data rates may apply.

Opt out and help

Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help. After you text STOP, we will not send further campaign messages to that number.

Carriers and processors

Messages are sent through our SMS provider (Twilio). Carriers and Twilio may process the number and message metadata to deliver the text.
