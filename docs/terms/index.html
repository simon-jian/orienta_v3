Last updated: September 13, 2026

These terms cover SMS invites sent by Orienta, operated by Zhenxiang Jian (sole proprietor). By saying yes to an invite text at the airport desk, you agree to receive that message.

What the messages are

Orienta SMS is transactional. A typical message is a one-time link so you can open your trip on the phone that received it. It is not advertising.

The operator will tell you they can text a one-time Orienta link, that you will usually receive one message for the trip, that message and data rates may apply, and that you can reply HELP or STOP. They will ask yes or no. They send the SMS only if you say yes.

Message frequency and rates

Frequency: one invite SMS per trip, plus a resend only if requested. Message and data rates may apply.
